WarrantyOS design system · 03

AI visual language

Three actors, three treatments. AI output is always labelled, tinted violet and dashed-bordered — a proposal. Human decisions are blue and solid — committed. System actions are grey and carry timestamps.

AI recommends
Violet ink, tinted surface, dashed border, “AI” tag, confidence always shown. Words: recommends, suggests, detected — never “is”.
Human approves
Blue ink, solid border, person’s name and time. Overrides show AI value struck-through beside the human value.
System executes
Neutral grey, monospace IDs and timestamps. Shown only after guardrails pass or a human decides.

Rules
1. AI content is never styled as final. 2. Confidence is always a number, never colour alone. 3. Reasoning is shown as factors, evidence and citations — not a transcript. 4. Human override is a primary control, never an error state. 5. Tenant and policy version always appear next to AI output.

Accessibility

• Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on every actor surface (violet ink #563B75 on #F0EBF5 = 8.1:1).
• Status never conveyed by colour alone — every badge has text.
• 2px focus ring, 2px offset, on all interactive elements.
• Keyboard: ⌘K palette, Esc closes overlays, tab order follows reading order.
• Dialogs trap focus and return it to the trigger.
• Charts have text equivalents; motion respects reduced-motion.
• Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile.

Responsive behaviour

Desktop ≥1280 — 240px sidebar, two- and three-column workspaces, tables.
Tablet 768–1279 — sidebar collapses to 64px rail; AI panel becomes a drawer; tables scroll horizontally.
Mobile <768 — task-first: tracking, notifications, review/approve, evidence viewing, service updates. Tables become cards; navigation is a bottom bar; approve/reject sit in a sticky action sheet.
