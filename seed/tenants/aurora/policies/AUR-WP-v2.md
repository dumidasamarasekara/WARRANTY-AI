# Aurora Limited Warranty (version 2)

## AUR-WP-1.1 Coverage — manufacturing defects

Aurora Electronics warrants Aurora tablets, phones and laptops against defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use during the warranty period. A defect is covered when the product fails
to perform as designed — for example it does not power on, does not charge, the display or touch
input fails, or a built-in component stops working — and the failure was not caused by an event
excluded in section 3.

## AUR-WP-1.2 Definitions

"Purchase date" is the date on the original proof of purchase. "Region" is the region where the
product was purchased; when it cannot be determined, the region of the customer's address applies.
"Battery" means the built-in rechargeable battery and its charging circuitry.

## AUR-WP-2.1 Warranty period — North America

For products purchased in North America, manufacturing defects are covered for 12 months from the
purchase date. Claims for defects that first appear after the 12-month period are not covered.

## AUR-WP-2.2 Warranty period — European Union

For products purchased in the European Union, manufacturing defects are covered for 24 months from
the purchase date. Claims for defects that first appear after the 24-month period are not covered.

## AUR-WP-2.3 Warranty period — battery

The battery is covered for 12 months from the purchase date in all regions. A battery that no longer
holds a charge, swells or fails to charge after 12 months is not covered, even if the rest of the
product is still within its warranty period.

## AUR-WP-3.1 Exclusions — accidental damage

Damage caused by accidents is not covered, including drops, impacts, cracked or shattered screens,
bent housings and damage from pressure or crushing.

## AUR-WP-3.2 Exclusions — liquid damage

Damage caused by contact with liquid is not covered, including spills, submersion, condensation and
corrosion of internal components caused by moisture.

## AUR-WP-3.3 Exclusions — cosmetic damage

Cosmetic damage that does not affect the function of the product is not covered, including
scratches, dents, scuffs and discoloration.

## AUR-WP-3.4 Exclusions — unauthorized repair

Damage caused by, and any defect following, a repair, modification or opening of the product by
anyone other than Aurora or an Aurora-authorized service center is not covered.

## AUR-WP-4.1 Remedy

For a covered defect, Aurora will, at its option, repair the product at an authorized service
center or replace it with an equivalent product. Repaired or replacement products are covered for
the remainder of the original warranty period.

## AUR-WP-4.2 Making a claim

A claim must include the product model and serial number, the original proof of purchase, a
description of the problem and photos showing the product, its serial label and the problem. Aurora
may request missing or illegible information before deciding the claim.
